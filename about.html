About

I work at the intersection of applied systems engineering, embedded hardware, and practical automation. My focus is on building reliable, maintainable systems that help bring manufacturing back to pragmatic, resilient local production — not for novelty, but to rebuild supply chains and reduce systemic fragility.

Driving goals

  • Reindustrialization of America: design tools, modules, and processes that make local manufacturing economically and technically viable.
  • Automation of maintenance: reduce mean time to repair through better telemetry, modular repairable designs, and software that surfaces real problems and executes it.
  • Optimization of software: pragmatic, performance-aware code that matches constraints of embedded systems and production tooling.

Hands-on interests

I enjoy projects that mix visual design with hardware — from PCB layout and embedded firmware to polished UI assets and system diagrams. My hobbies and toolset include:

  • Graphics and visualization for UI and documentation
  • Embedded devices and microcontroller firmware
  • PCB design with manufacturability in mind
  • Applied chemical engineering where it intersects manufacturing and materials

If any of this resonates — collaboration, consulting, or a technical chat — drop me a line via the contact page.
